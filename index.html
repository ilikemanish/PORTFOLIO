Smoking Health Risk Analysis

Power BI · DAX · 2,500 Patient Records
View Repo

The Problem

Healthcare datasets often hide critical risk patterns behind flat tables. The goal was to build an interactive, filter-first dashboard that lets stakeholders explore smoking exposure, organ damage conditions, and multi-dimensional health indicators across 2,500 patient records.

My Approach

I modelled a star-schema Power BI report with a health_dataset.csv fact table (13 fields, 2,500 rows, 0 nulls) plus dimension tables for condition and organ. I built a DAX Age Group column and two comparison measures — vs Avg Age and vs Avg BMI.

Key Insights

  • Smoking split: Never 45.4%, Current 29.3%, Former 25.2%.
  • Exposure contrast: Current smokers avg 16.8 cig/day and 22.2 yrs.
  • Organ damage range: Only 5 pts spread (Liver 37.7% → Human Body 32.7%).

Online Book Store Analysis

A PostgreSQL deep dive into 20 business questions across books, customers, orders, and inventory.

01

The Problem

The store had plenty of raw data but no single source of truth. Which genres sell best? Which inventory is running low?

02

The Approach

Progressive SQL from joins to correlated subqueries.

SQL
SELECT c.name, SUM(o.total_amount) AS spend
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o ON o.customer_id = c.id
GROUP BY c.name
ORDER BY spend DESC LIMIT 5;
