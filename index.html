Blinkit Sales & Outlet Performance

Power BI · DAX · Retail Analytics
View Repo

The Problem

Blinkit's grocery data spans multiple dimensions — outlet types, locations, product categories, and customer ratings. Stakeholders needed a filter-first dashboard to explore $1.20M in sales across 8,523 items, identify top-performing segments, and switch KPIs without rebuilding the page.

My Approach

I built a single-page Power BI report with a filter panel (Outlet Location, Outlet Size, Item Type), a Metrics field parameter to switch between Total Sales, Avg Sales, No. of Items, and Avg Rating, plus donut charts, bar charts, a funnel chart, line chart, and a multi-metric outlet-type matrix.

Key Insights

  • Regular-fat products dominate sales at 64.6% ($776.32K) vs Low Fat at 35.4%.
  • Medium outlets are the largest size segment at 42.3% ($507.90K).
  • Tier 3 locations contribute the largest location share at 39.3%.
  • Supermarket Type1 dominates outlet format at 65.5% ($787.55K).
